_What follows was a short commentary I chose to write for myself expand on a thought I had and I decided to post it here anyways. It avoids any political and ideological identification and bias but highlights specifically US politics to communicate a point that emotions are being weaponized, and regardless of the purpose, we should reclaim some sanity and civility by recognizing that this weaponization is ever present in today's society._

The problem with most political reporting, especially the smaller media outlets pushing 'clips' and focusing on social media and youtube, are that they're not only making overly strong statements (not quiet click-bait) but they're making statements on micro indicators; not too different from stock prediction but without the ability to gain any semblance fo value from the fluctuations. These small gaffs, back and forth negotiations, news headlines, and even questionably legal actions are all getting washed away by information overload and the stress of modern world
issues long before any of them can permanently mar any political figure or party (in most cases, beyond the general trend of party lean).

These statements by news media, sometimes more justly communicated as predictions, are often delusionally optimistic that perceived consequences will occur in their favor. And even if there's a moderate expection of those consequences occurring, the timelines for changes in politics or judicial responses are feeling unimaginably slow due to the never ending flows of attention and time sucking media we're bombarded with daily, designed to weaponize your thoughts and opinions to sell you things and ideas.

Changing your mindset and understanding, and doing so consistently, can help you to see things more rationally and stop reacting so emotionally. Sometimes this may require avoiding sources that trigger irrational responses from you, but manipulation doesn't stop with the things you can control. People themselves are taking advantage of biological imperatives and nature, things beyond our immediate control physiologically, like refusing to dress modestly for the narcisistic control of gaze and attention. The point being made is to not pay it attention, actively ignore
it, and respectfully go on with your day. And back to the political spectrum, not one person fits wholely into the ideals of the political party they're voting for and neither party is aligned on an individual level; so baiting the opposite party's voters in the guise of party pride or getting triggered by politically affiliated branding is beyond obserd. 

My point is that people need to stop being so reactionary, and the first step is to change the lens you view society to maintain rational thought by disarming the all of the weaponized thoughts, ideas, and messaging forced upon us.
